Money-movement quality and funnel

What this measures, and what it does not. Payins and payouts only — the movements with a lifecycle, an external counterparty and a way to fail. Trading obligations and charges post or they do not; they have no funnel and are not on this page.

Every figure here is derived. One array of movements, one metrics() function. Nothing on this page is stored or hardcoded — the same rule app.js applies to balances.

The reason codes are not invented. They are the outcome ids the funds flow already emits, so the dashboard can only report failures the product is actually able to produce.
